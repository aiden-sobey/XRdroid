# XRdroid Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

XRdroid ("the app") is an Android app that places app screens in positions around you when you wear VITURE glasses, and lets you control them with your phone. It is published by Aiden Sobey ("I", "me").

## Summary

XRdroid does not collect, store off your phone, share or sell any personal data. It has no internet access.

## No network access

The app does not request the Android internet permission and contains no code that sends data anywhere. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash reporting. Nothing the app handles leaves your phone.

## What the app uses, and why

All of the following stays on your phone and is used only to make the app work:

- **Installed apps.** The app reads the list of apps that can be launched, so it can show them in the home screen's dock and app drawer. The list is not stored or sent anywhere.
- **App usage access.** The app uses Android's usage access permission to rank the dock by how often you open apps. Only the resulting dock order is kept.
- **Motion sensor on the glasses.** Head tracking reads the glasses' motion sensor over USB, live, to keep the screens in place. This data is not recorded or stored.
- **Shizuku.** The app uses Shizuku, a separate app that you install and control, to run a helper on your phone that creates the screens, opens apps on them and delivers touches. The helper runs only on your phone and does not communicate with any server. Shizuku has its own privacy practices, described at https://shizuku.rikka.app.

## What the app stores

The app keeps these on your phone, in its private storage:

- Your settings, such as the chosen layout, screen size and cursor mode.
- Layouts you create.
- Your dock choices.
- A background image, if you choose one.

Android removes all of this when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes, I will update this policy and its effective date before releasing the change.
